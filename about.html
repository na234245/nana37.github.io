ABOUT

我是一名视觉创作者，专注于摄影、影像剪辑以及视觉叙事。

I am a visual creator focusing on photography, film editing and visual storytelling.

摄影对我而言，不只是按下快门的瞬间， 更是一种观察世界的方法。

For me, photography is not only the moment of pressing the shutter, but also a way of observing and understanding the world.

我喜欢寻找日常中的细节： 一束穿过窗户的光， 城市街角的人影， 陌生人与空间之间短暂的连接。

I enjoy discovering overlooked details in everyday life: a beam of light passing through a window, a figure standing on a city street, and the brief connection between people and spaces.

我的作品受到摄影、电影、音乐与文学的影响。 我希望通过不同媒介之间的结合， 创造具有情绪和故事感的视觉作品。

My work is influenced by photography, cinema, music and literature. Through the combination of different mediums, I hope to create visual works with emotion and narrative.

未来，我希望持续探索影像的可能性， 记录真实、克制而有温度的瞬间。

In the future, I hope to continue exploring the possibilities of visual expression, capturing authentic, subtle and warm moments.

—— NANA KUI
